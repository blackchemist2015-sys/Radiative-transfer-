::: {.titlepage}
**Lectures SA1–SA5**, final exam (SA6) and formula sheet (SA7). Prerequisite: Course A, *Radiative Transfer* (RT1–RT6). Every lecture opens with a concept map and ends with misconceptions, a summary and four exam questions with answers.

**Based on:** E. Novotny, *Introduction to Stellar Atmospheres and Interiors* (1973) · D. Mihalas, *Stellar Atmospheres* (1978) · G. W. Collins II, *Fundamentals of Stellar Astrophysics*, Part II · D. F. Gray, *The Observation and Analysis of Stellar Photospheres* · S. Chandrasekhar, *Radiative Transfer* (1950) · Hubeny & Mihalas, *Theory of Stellar Atmospheres* (2014)

**Code:** figures and numbers come from `scripts/lte_solvers.py`, `scripts/sa_physics.py` and `scripts/worked_examples.py`.
:::
