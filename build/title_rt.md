**Lectures RT1–RT6**, final exam (RT7) and formula sheet (RT8). Every lecture opens with a concept map, explains each idea in words before the mathematics, and ends with misconceptions, a summary and four exam questions with answers. Course B, *Stellar Atmospheres*, applies this theory to stars.

**Based on:** A670 *Advanced Astrophysics* Lecture 1 · W. Hu, *Astro 305 Notes, Set 1* · MIT 8.901 (2019) Lecture 7 · G. W. Collins II, *Fundamentals of Stellar Astrophysics*, Ch. 9 · S. Chandrasekhar, *Radiative Transfer* (1950) · E. Novotny, *Introduction to Stellar Atmospheres and Interiors* (1973)

**Code:** all figures and numbers are reproducible with the Python scripts in `scripts/`. The interactive web app is in `app/` and the spreadsheet is `Radiative_Transfer_Lab.xlsx`.
:::
